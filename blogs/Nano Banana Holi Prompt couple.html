Happy Holi Ai Photo Editing Prompts 2026 [Google Gemini] For Couples

Posted On: 📅Feb 25, 2026

Reading Time: ⏱️10 minutes


📌 How to use: Upload reference image first. ➜ Paste any of the below prompt in Google Gemini AI using Nano Banana/Nano Banana Pro. ➜ BOOM! Your Image is Ready.

PROMPT 1

Use the uploaded male photo and uploaded female photo as the PRIMARY and ONLY face references. Both faces must match 100% exactly — same identity, facial structure, skin tone, eyes, nose, lips, hair, and expressions. Do NOT change, stylize, or beautify the faces. NO anime, NO cartoon, NO illustration style. Faces must look fully realistic and photographic. Create a realistic modern Indian Holi celebration scene with a young couple standing close together in a romantic yet tasteful pose. Female look: – Wearing a black crop top or fitted t-shirt with colorful Holi powder – Natural makeup, soft smile – Face and hair lightly covered with gulal colors – Holding a small brush or color naturally Male look: – Wearing a black t-shirt with custom name text like “Karan” / “Karan” (editable) – Casual jeans – Standing close behind the girl in a gentle, caring pose Holi details: – Colorful gulal on clothes, hands, and faces – Soft rain or mist effect (optional) – People celebrating Holi in the background – Outdoor garden or open field setting – Background slightly blurred for cinematic focus Photography & Quality: – DSLR camera realism – Cinematic natural lighting – Ultra-realistic skin texture – Sharp focus on both faces – Shallow depth of field – High dynamic range (HDR) – 4K ultra-HD quality Mood: – Romantic – Youthful – Festive – Stylish Holi vibe Final output must look like a REAL professional photograph, not AI generated.

PROMPT 2

Create an ultra-realistic, cinematic romantic couple portrait captured mid-Holi celebration. The couple is caught in perfectly synchronized motion, joyfully flinging colorful Holi powder at each other, with dramatic bursts of neon pinks, electric blues, and citrus yellows frozen mid-air in crisp detail. They share intimate eye contact with natural, joyful laughter, expressing romance, chemistry, and playful festival energy. Soft sunlight and gentle sun-flare accents enhance the vibrancy of the colors, while maintaining natural skin tones. Their clothing is artistically blended with Holi pigments, showing realistic fabric texture, folds, and powder absorption, creating a natural yet stylish festive look. Shot on a professional DSLR camera with cinematic framing inspired by trending Instagram reels, 85mm lens look, f/1.8 aperture, shallow depth of field for creamy background bokeh and strong subject separation. Ultra-HD 4K clarity, high dynamic range, vibrant yet balanced color grading, subtle cinematic film grain for texture, smooth motion blur on flying powder particles, romantic yet energetic Holi mood. No face distortion, no beautification filters, no cartoon or painterly effects, no AI artifacts, no text, no watermark, no logo.

PROMPT 3

Wide holistic portrait of a couple throwing rainbow Holi powder together toward camera, ultra-HD, sunlight glinting off colorful particles, warm and bold color grading, intertwined hands and joyful expressions, matching stylish outfits mixed with vivid pigments, soft hazy bokeh background, trending Instagram joyful festival aesthetic with color pop enhancement and subtle vignette.
